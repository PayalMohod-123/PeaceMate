🕊️ PeaceMate – AI-Based Mental Wellness Chatbot
PeaceMate is an AI-powered mental wellness chatbot designed to provide emotional support in a simple, private, and judgment-free environment.
The project focuses on helping users manage stress, express emotions, and improve mental well-being through mindful AI-driven conversations.
📄 Problem Statement
In today’s fast-paced lifestyle, people across all age groups experience increasing levels of stress, emotional imbalance, and mental fatigue.
Many individuals hesitate to openly share their feelings due to social stigma, lack of awareness, or fear of judgment.
Additionally, professional mental health support is not always easily accessible, affordable, or available at the time of need.
There is a growing requirement for a simple, private, and easily accessible digital platform that can offer emotional support and promote mental well-being for everyone.
💡 Proposed Solution
PeaceMate is an AI-based mental wellness chatbot that provides:
⦁	A safe and judgment-free space for emotional expression
⦁	Calm, mindful, and supportive AI responses
⦁	Easy accessibility without social pressure
⦁	A focus on simplicity, user comfort, and emotional balance
The system acts as a supportive companion for users who hesitate to seek traditional mental health support.

🌍 Multilingual & Voice Support (Technical Note)
PeaceMate supports multilingual text responses, including English, Hindi, Marathi, and Punjabi.
Voice output depends on browser-based Text-to-Speech (TTS) engines.
Currently, most browsers offer stable voice support mainly for English, while Indian languages have limited native support.
This is a browser-level limitation, not an application issue.
